## 1. One line

**Orbit is the professional network inside your building.** Every week it finds 3 people nearby worth meeting and helps you book a coffee with them.

**Brand line:** *AI does the awkward middle. You just show up.*

**Why the name:** the most useful people are already in your orbit, a few floors away or a two-minute walk across the road. Orbit pulls them close.

---

## 2. The problem

You share a building with hundreds of people you should know, and you'll meet almost none of them.

- People badge in, go to their floor, work and leave. Hallway serendipity is gone, and hybrid work has thinned it further.
- The co-founder, first customer, mentor or hire you need is often two floors away, and you'll never know.
- **LinkedIn** is global and built for broadcasting. Proximity means nothing to it, and it rarely turns into a real conversation.
- **Events and mixers** are sporadic and awkward, and they attract the same few extroverts.
- **Building WhatsApp groups and member directories** are passive. You have to browse and cold-message, so almost nobody does.

**The gap:** nothing proactively introduces you to the *specific* people near you who are worth your time and takes the awkwardness out of reaching out.

**The third axis of networking:** people network by *who they've worked with* (history) and *who they're linked to* (LinkedIn). Nobody networks by **where they physically are**. Orbit does.

## 3. What Orbit is (and isn't)

| Orbit is | Orbit is not |
|---|---|
| 3 curated intros a week | A feed, timeline or endless scroll |
| Matched both ways on what you give and what you want | A directory you have to browse |
| A real coffee, in person, nearby | Chat, DMs or likes |
| Mutual opt-in: nothing happens unless both say yes | Public profiles on display |
| Private: your phone is shared only after both confirm | A place to broadcast |

## 4. How it works (live today)

1. **Sign up with your email.** No password. You get a one-time magic link that expires in 20 minutes.
2. **Fill in a 2-minute profile:**
   - your building, name, role, company, floor and seniority;
   - your **LinkedIn** and **phone (WhatsApp)**, both required;
   - **"I can give"** and **"I want"** tags, such as product strategy, fundraising advice, design feedback, a technical co-founder or first customers.
3. **Set your free times.** A simple weekly grid of one-hour blocks, Mon–Fri, 10 am–6 pm IST.
4. **Get 3 intros every week.** Each card shows:
   - the person's role and company;
   - a match percentage;
   - a one-line **"why you two"** reason, for example: *"Meera can help with content & growth, and you're a strong source of product strategy. A genuinely two-way coffee."*
5. **Propose a coffee.** Choose:
   - **Length:** 30 minutes or 1 hour.
   - **When:** from the other person's free times. Solid slots mean you're free too; dashed slots mean you'd adjust your day. You can also suggest any other time.
   - **Where to meet:** DLF cafeteria, CDS 2nd floor or CDS canteen.
6. **They reply.** They get an email and can **confirm**, **decline** or **suggest another time and place**. If they don't reply within 24 hours, Orbit sends them a reminder.
7. **Confirmed.** Both people get an email with the time, the place, a Google Calendar link and **each other's phone number and WhatsApp link** to coordinate. Each person's LinkedIn is shown on the meeting page.
8. **Day before:** both get a reminder. **After:** a quick "did you meet? was it worth it?" check-in.

## 5. Who it's for

### Members, the professionals in the building (free)
Founders, operators, product people, designers, engineers, GTM and sales leaders, investors and consultants: anyone whose work benefits from the right introduction.

- **Pain:** short on time, dislikes networking theatre and cold outreach, won't maintain another feed.
- **Promise:** meet genuinely useful people with almost no effort. They're right here, so a coffee costs 30 minutes, not a commute.

### Buyers, building operators (paid)
- Coworking operators (WoCo, Awfis, Smartworks, 91springboard, Innov8, Table Space).
- Office-tower and business-park landlords and management (DLF, CDS, Capital Tower).
- Workplace, community and tenant-experience teams.

**Why they pay:**
- **Tenant retention:** churn is expensive.
- **Leasing differentiation:** every building has wifi and a gym; few can say "our tenants actually meet each other".
- **Proof of amenity value:** real engagement data to justify spend.

**Model:** B2B2C. Operators pay; their tenants use Orbit free as an amenity.

## 8. Messaging

### Taglines
- **The professional network inside your building** (primary; used on Product Hunt)
- Meet the 3 people in your building worth knowing this week (site headline)
- Your next collaborator is in the building
- 3 people in your building worth a coffee, every week
- Meet the people two floors away
- AI does the awkward middle. You just show up.

### Short descriptions
- **Under 160 characters:** Every week, Orbit finds 3 people in your building worth a coffee, matched on what you give and what you want. Free for members.
- **About 50 words:** Orbit is the professional network for the people inside your building. Every week it suggests 3 people nearby worth meeting, matched both ways on what you can give and what you want. Pick a time from their free hours and a spot to meet. Once they confirm, you swap numbers.
- **For operators:** Orbit turns your building into a community. Every week each tenant gets 3 useful introductions to people in the building and books a real coffee. You get an engagement dashboard that proves the amenity works. ₹15,000 per building per month.

### Key benefits
- **Zero effort:** no browsing, no cold DMs. Three intros arrive each week.
- **Two-way value:** every match is someone who can help you *and* whom you can help.
- **Real, in person:** a 30-minute coffee a short walk away, not another online connection.
- **Respectful and private:** mutual opt-in; numbers shared only after both confirm.
- **Fits your calendar:** pick from their free times, choose the length and place, counter-offer in one tap.

### Proof points (product facts)
- Magic-link sign-in with no passwords; tokens are single-use and expire in 20 minutes.
- Times shown in India time; 30-minute or 1-hour coffees; three meeting spots.
- Automatic emails for invites, confirmations, declines, reschedules, 24-hour nudges and day-before reminders.
- An operator dashboard with an activation funnel and show-up rate.

### Voice and tone
Warm, calm, human, a little premium. Short sentences. Talk about people and coffee, not "AI-powered networking solutions". Never hype-y, never feed-like. The anti-feed stance is the brand.

**Avoid:** "social network", "connect with thousands", "grow your network", "swipe", "followers".

## 9. FAQ

- **Is it free?** Yes, for members. Building operators pay.
- **Who can see my profile?** Only people Orbit introduces you to. Nothing is public. Your phone number is shown only to someone you've both confirmed a coffee with.
- **What if I don't want to meet someone?** Pass on the intro or decline the coffee. Nobody is notified that you passed.
- **What if their times don't suit me?** Pick one of their slots anyway if you can adjust, or suggest your own time and place.
- **How are matches made?** Orbit compares what each person can give with what the other wants, in both directions. It adds small bonuses for complementary seniority and nearby floors, then picks the top 3 for the week.
- **Which buildings?** DLF Tower, CDS, WoCo and Capital Tower in Gurugram for now.
- **How do I bring Orbit to my building?** Use "Get Orbit for your building" on the site, or contact us. Operators get an invite link and a QR code for the lobby.

## 11. Metrics we care about

- **Activation:** a member gets a confirmed coffee within 7 days of signing up. This is our north-star early metric.
- **Funnel:** onboarded → got intros → proposed a coffee → confirmed → actually met.
- **Show-up rate** and **"worth it" rate**, from post-coffee check-ins.
- **Density per building:** matching gets noticeably better past about 15–20 active members.
